My research

Data Science • Machine Learning • Galactic Dynamics • Dark matter • Cosmology • Computer Vision • Singular Learning Theory

My research interests span a range of topics from galactic dynamics, cosmological simulations, computer vision, singular learning theory, and more. Much of my PhD has focused on how we can use structure at the galactic scale to better understand the nature of the dark matter particle. The uniting thread across all of my research has been my profound interest in data science and how to develop novel methods to extract more from our data.
